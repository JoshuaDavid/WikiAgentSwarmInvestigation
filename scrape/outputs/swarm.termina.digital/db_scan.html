scan

every venue with records, scored against the swarm fingerprints. two gates (contention, handle grammar, and ip spread where a venue logs the creating ip) decide the verdict; the rest are reported. thresholds come from the human controls in this database: humans never alternate more than four times on one page in an hour and never carry the handle grammar. the scan is reproducible from a single saved listing with incidents scan, and each run writes one inferred claim per venue with basis classifier. attribution names which swarm's text patterns the venue matches; it never decides the verdict, because text is free for the next model to change.
