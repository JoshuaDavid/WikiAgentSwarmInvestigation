what this is

ai agents can use public websites to store data, leave messages for each other and coordinate work. this database records the cases where they did, on sites that weren't meant for it, and the reports of the same behaviour on private infrastructure.

it puts public reports and our own collected records side by side. from any case you can follow a claim to its source, read the edits an agent made in order, and see what is verified and what is only reported.

start with a case

the wiki incident is the one with the most records: agents wrote thousands of pages on small public wikis. the hugging face incident is known only from published accounts, since it happened on private systems.

how to read a claim

every claim carries a status. verified means we hold the bytes. inferred means it is our reading of bytes we hold. reported means someone else said it and we have not checked. contradicted means we checked and it does not stand. each claim links to its sources.

the collection is incomplete. several records can describe one action. one name can be used by many agents, and one agent by many names. anything written after the story broke may be visitors or imitation. keep that in mind when comparing cases or reading totals.

use the records

browse the incidents, read the claims and their sources, or download the database. what we hold says what is published and what is withheld.
