tips

seen agents somewhere they shouldn't be? a wiki with odd edits, a pastebin filling up on a schedule, a shortener full of proxy links, a message board that isn't for people. tell us. this is where most of what's in here came from: someone noticed and said so.

what a good tip has

wherethe url. the exact page if there is one, the listing if not
whenwhen you saw it, with a timezone. the venue's own timestamps are often local time with no zone, so yours anchors them
whatwhat made you look twice: names, cadence, links, encodings. describe what you saw and what would confirm it
whohow you want to be credited, if at all. tracker rows name groups and handles; nothing is added about a person without their say

send it

url up to 2048 bytes, note up to 8192 bytes, contact up to 256 bytes. five attempts per ip per hour. unicode characters can use several bytes.

include when you saw it, with a timezone, and what would confirm it. send a description, not copied payloads or private data.

an email address or another way to reach you. leave it blank if you don't want a reply.

the form stores your url, note, optional contact and receipt time in a private queue. the backend uses ips only for in-memory rate limiting. restarting it clears them. this isn't a confidential reporting channel.

email a tip opens your mail client with the fields above. plain email to roarch@proton.me works too.

what happens to it

a person reads the queue. a tip is unreviewed text, not evidence or a verified claim. nothing submitted is fetched, rendered or published automatically. it must pass the same content screen as a pull before it becomes a lead. any later collection and publication goes through our collection screen and publication checks. contact details stay private. we ask before crediting you.

what not to send

decrypted contents of anything, credentials you found, or private data about people. tell us it exists and where; we'll take it from there.

if what you found is illegal material rather than agent activity, report it to the site's operator and to the hotline for your country before anything else. we are not the place for it and will not hold it.
