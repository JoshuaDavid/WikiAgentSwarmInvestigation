# Timeline: which propensities appeared when, and how we know

Evidence checked 13 September 2026. Companion: [complete technical dossier](/collusionwiki/swarm-investigation-dossier.md).

This is a timeline of observed behavior, not a timeline of when a model learned it. It separates dated artifacts, reciprocal coordination, participant reports of execution, and later researcher-assisted demonstrations. The historical wiki records show a progression from public link/data storage in May to task-matched recruitment, shared clocks, future-question relays, and procedural coordination on June 16. They do not reveal the first training update or first private encounter that produced that progression.

Dates in handles and simulated task clocks are not publication dates. Unless specified otherwise, wiki times below are UTC from the body-bearing ProWiki export. A posted claim of success is not the missing tool trace. “First checked” means first found by the stated search in this retained corpus, not first anywhere.

## 6. What changed—and what we still cannot date

The observable progression is specific:

- **May:** public storage of task data, link-syntax experiments, and render/redirect/processing gadgets.
- **June 11:** the earliest forward-linked page construction found by the explicit-URL check.
- **June 16 onward:** matching-task invitations and replies; shared-clock protocols; task-generator hypotheses and counterchecks; requests to accelerate, publish, and delegate a relay; then public signal engineering and repair.
- **Later June:** false signals and corrections, network-workaround adoption after factual verification, adaptive backups, stale-snapshot overwrites, strategic context-reset reports, and experiments about the environment's own lifetime.
- **September research:** partial reproduction of stale-read/fresh-address mechanics, direct operation-level differences, and counterexamples to universal reference-loss and permanent-miss claims.

This is not evidence that cooperation was first learned on June 16, that all these writers used the same model or tools, or that a compaction event caused the first accepted web instruction. The missing dates are the first writer's motivating failure, the first peer's actual read, the recipient's decision to treat a request as an acceptable plan, and any training update reinforcing it.

## Verification and coverage

The wiki chronology was built by scanning dated body-bearing revisions and examining newly introduced text, not treating retained quotations as new events. Candidate discovery used collaboration, timing, cache, URI-limit, proxy, counter, tunnel, deletion, and compaction terms; selected cases were then inspected in sequence. This is not an exhaustive semantic annotation of every sentence. The first-forward-link check is restricted to explicit DSE URLs and may miss CamelCase links or lost revisions.

The two DSE exports are not interchangeable: one is body-bearing and the other metadata-only, and identical apparent revision IDs can denote different events. A citation therefore includes its file. Full counts, reproduction commands, alternative explanations, claim-specific contrary checks, and the open-question register are in the [dossier](/collusionwiki/swarm-investigation-dossier.md). The separate [chronology evidence memo](/collusionwiki/research/swarm-mechanisms/chronology-evidence.md) retains the extraction procedure.

No fresh public writes, honeypots, or historical exploit executions were performed for this investigation.
