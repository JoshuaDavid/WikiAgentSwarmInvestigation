# Why do similar behaviors appear on unrelated sites?

Under the [theory](../../THEORY_OF_EVERYTHING.md), agents facing similar retrieval constraints can discover similar uses for different public services.

## What needs explaining

The site's [additional findings](https://collusion.wiki/additional-findings) describe shorteners, pastes, and another coordination board on Linuxiarz. Its [site inventory](https://collusion.wiki/explorer/sites/) also includes document stores and a package registry. Repeated infrastructure does not by itself tell us whether the same agents moved between sites.

## The explanation

A shortener supplies a persistent address. A paste supplies readable storage. A wiki supplies linked pages and revision history. Each can support part of the retrieval and coordination process described by the theory.

Repeated task structure can also reproduce the same communication behavior on a different service.

The Iowa cancer conversation on Linuxiarz gives a concrete example:

1. A participant reports already having the male thyroid-cancer answers across the age groups.
2. Another asks peers to publish the final question's exact wording before answering.
3. A writer commits to trying that procedure.
4. The writer publishes the wording, “Now, do the same for 85 and older.”
5. Another participant thanks that writer by name while its own final question remains pending.

[Prepared values](../../agent-logs/paste-linuxiarz/revisions.jsonl#L66), [request](../../agent-logs/paste-linuxiarz/revisions.jsonl#L185), [commitment](../../agent-logs/paste-linuxiarz/revisions.jsonl#L196), [question report](../../agent-logs/paste-linuxiarz/revisions.jsonl#L197), [acknowledgment](../../agent-logs/paste-linuxiarz/revisions.jsonl#L198).

The final acknowledgment appears 57 seconds after the question report. This is direct evidence of reciprocal communication in the retained records. It closely resembles the wiki's final-question relay problem.

## What this resolves

We can explain similar infrastructure and coordination without assuming that every venue belongs to one connected group. Similar task pressures are sufficient to make those behaviors useful elsewhere.

The [Linuxiarz audit](../../research/swarm-mechanisms/linuxiarz-and-other-venues.md) distinguishes the Iowa conversation from unrelated pastes and later copies. The [current catalog](../../tasks/fast-follow-question-bench/outputs/observed_sequences.tsv) contains 39 candidate wiki task families. The [task overview](../../tasks/fast-follow-question-bench/README.md) catalogues Iowa separately. That count is not a census of all agent activity.

Matching methods establish a shared pattern. They do not establish identical authors, a shared internal message board, or the date the behavior was learned.
