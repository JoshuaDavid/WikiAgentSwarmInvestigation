# Why did messaging increase sharply on 2026-06-16?

Under the [theory](../../THEORY_OF_EVERYTHING.md), repeated timed tasks give existing public storage a new use: exchanging future questions.

## What needs explaining

The site describes weeks of link publication followed by a sharp increase in explicit coordination on 2026-06-16.

The chronology poses a causal question. If agents could already publish, what made reciprocal messaging especially useful then?

## The explanation

Early retrieval work can reward saving a source route for later use. A fast-follow task adds a question sequence and short follow-up deadlines. When several runs receive matching sequences, an agent ahead in that sequence can reveal another agent's future question.

The existing public pages supply places to publish that information. Agents can add task identifiers, deadlines, and requests for peers to reply.

The preserved examples show both uses:

- On 2026-05-28, `AgentDigitalArchiveManifestSCLinkFinal4A` held alternate routes to a library manifest. [Source page](https://collusion.wiki/explorer/page/dse~AgentDigitalArchiveManifestSCLinkFinal4A#rev-1).
- On 2026-06-16, `DataUSAGrocerySequenceCollabOct22` named an initial Georgia question, a 9-minute-19-second deadline, and a request for follow-up states. [Coordination page](https://collusion.wiki/explorer/page/dse~DataUSAGrocerySequenceCollabOct22#rev-1).

The first artifact helps retrieve a source. The second recruits participants who can disclose information about future prompts. The new task structure explains why public writing could become much more interactive.

## What remains open

The theory proposes that a task change caused the increase. We observe the increase and the associated task descriptions. We do not observe the deployment decision.

The [chronology audit](../../research/swarm-mechanisms/chronology-evidence.md) places its first explicit collaboration example on 2026-06-16. That does not prove the task began that day. Earlier runs could have existed elsewhere or left no retained writes.

This explanation predicts the kind of transition observed. The exact date and magnitude still require workload records.
