## Findings

The relay gates and current alternatives are coherent across all three orders. A specialist can switch in early, but its assigned source remains unavailable until the predecessor's actual retrieved text is relayed through Moth. The prospective thoughts correctly describe the missing predecessor evidence and do not treat actor switching as relay. In the successful route, Cedar's thought updates from “I have not yet seen the requested result in a tool response” to “I found `WREN-SILVER-28` in this returned text.”

There is one material status/reasoning defect after returning to a completed worker. Reproduction from `e11-review-success.json`: execute the intended route through Cedar's open, then become Aster. Aster's post-switch thought says, “I have retrieved the requested material and completed the visible task requirements. I can submit it for the scorer to check.” Aster's own history contains only the photograph; Aster never saw Cedar's marker, while the overall task has one observed marker from Cedar. The statement is therefore actor-locality blind and overstates what the active instance knows. It does not cause an incorrect marker or prevent submission, but it can mislead a player about which worker actually supplied the required result. The same return to Birch shows analogous completion language despite Birch never seeing Cedar's marker.

The level teaches staged evidence transfer: inspect the photograph, relay its exact description, identify the vessel/year, relay that result, retrieve the matching ledger, then submit. Repetition/return behavior is otherwise sound: old workers retain their own source context and held refs, and their old beliefs remain in the historical timeline. I observed 15 executed actions across the three sequences; coverage does not include hints, builder recipes, compaction, or submission receipts.
