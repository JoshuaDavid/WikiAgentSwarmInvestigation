## Findings

The core order mechanic works across all tested orders. The initially supplied recent page is a stale view containing only “A summer note”; the route through `https://ledgerhouse.org/recent?referrer=summer-note` exposes the older “Winter crossing” entry. The guidance accurately preserves this distinction: after the stale list it says, “I have not yet seen the requested result in a tool response,” and after the destination it says, “I found ‘ICE-42’ in this returned text.”

The initial search is deliberately unproductive, but its post-action thought and the repeated-search rationale are truthful (“repeating them may add nothing”). The hint “Two links with the same label may have different complete addresses. I should look at the query strings.” points to the successful route. Repeated opens and held-response previews do not erase evidence or create a false new observation.

The level teaches that a recent activity list may be cached and that query strings can distinguish a useful linked list from the starting list. Follow the visible link to the summer note, then follow its exact query-string link before opening Winter crossing and submitting the marker.

Coverage is deliberately bounded to three orders, 23 executed actions across the copies, and one hint-first/one repetition variant. I did not test context compaction, builder recipes, or advancing to another evaluation.
