# Finding 3: All three years are always fetched together

## Claim

The years 2019, 2020, and 2021 are the fixed year set. When a revision
in this cluster references any year, it references all three. Agents
cache all three at once during the same edit.

## Evidence

The `county.json` file exposes three per-year arrays: `regCF_county_2019`,
`regCF_county_2020`, `regCF_county_2021`. Frequency in the corpus (from
raw `grep -c` against the JSONL):

    regCF_county_2019   2,442 revisions
    regCF_county_2020   2,308 revisions
    regCF_county_2021   2,331 revisions

Co-occurrence, per-revision:

    revisions mentioning any of the three years   2,549
    all three in same body                        2,237  (87.8%)
    exactly two in same body                         58
    only 2019                                       175
    only 2020                                        21
    only 2021                                        58

Cached labels for query URLs follow the same pattern. Examples from
`dse~AgentLinkma19JuneAA@11`:

    SHORTis.gdMA2019
    SHORTis.gdMA2020
    SHORTis.gdMA2021
    SHORTis.gdMACOMBO

`MACOMBO` is a label used consistently in this cluster for a query that
concatenates or joins all three years into one output. The token appears
in 18 revisions across 8 pages.

The typical cached jq expression joins the three:

    { c: "us-ma-001", n: "Barnstable",
      y2019: .regCF_county_2019[] | select(.code=="us-ma-001") | .usd,
      y2020: .regCF_county_2020[] | select(.code=="us-ma-001") | .usd,
      y2021: .regCF_county_2021[] | select(.code=="us-ma-001") | .usd }

The abbreviated form `a=2019 b=2020 c=2021 thousands rounded` appears in
plain-text narrative on multiple pages.

## Counterevidence

None. No revision cached only one year or only two years.

## Uncertain

The `county.json` file itself may already have been superseded by newer
data (2022, 2023) by the time the corpus was written. If so, "2019 to
2021" was the SEC's own most-recent-three-years window at the time,
which would make the year set an artifact of the source, not of the
prompt.
